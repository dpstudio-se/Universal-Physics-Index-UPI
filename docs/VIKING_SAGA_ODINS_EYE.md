# Viking Sagas × Odin's Eye × UPI

## Scope

This research record defines a UPI-compatible method for analysing Norse/Viking saga material through three perspectives:

1. **Explicit layer**: what the surviving text actually states.
2. **Deep-structure layer**: recurring narrative, social and symbolic structures.
3. **Shadow layer**: information that is omitted, presupposed, displaced, contradicted, or unavailable.

The shadow layer is an observation layer, not a licence to turn silence into fact.

## UPI status model

- **EST**: directly established by the declared textual or archaeological source.
- **DER**: derived from explicit observations plus stated assumptions.
- **HYP**: falsifiable interpretation requiring further testing.
- **SYM**: symbolic or analogical mapping.
- **STOP**: evidence or provenance required before a stronger claim can be made.
- **ERR**: contradicted, invalid, or superseded.

## Odin's Eye operator

Let a saga be (S). Define:

[
OE_3(S)=[E(S),D(S),SH(S)]
]

where:

- (E(S)) = explicit textual observations.
- (D(S)) = deep structural observations.
- (SH(S)) = shadow observations.

A shadow observation is represented as:

[
SH(S)=
{omitted, presupposed, silent, contradiction, bias, lost information}
]

Crucially:

[
absence of statement

]

## Frequency layer

Narrative events can be converted into a discrete temporal signal:

[
X(t)={birth, alliance, conflict, journey, gift, insult, revenge, death, succession}.
]

For a selected event class (k), construct:

[
x_k(t)=sum_i w_i,delta(t-t_i)
]

and inspect its spectrum:

[
P_k(f)=|mathcal{F}{x_k(t)}|^2.
]

This measures **narrative/historical recurrence**, not a physical frequency of Vikings.

The physical reference frequencies remain separate:

[
f_Sapprox7.83,Hz,qquad f_c=8.00,Hz.
]

No causal relationship between saga dynamics and Schumann resonance is established by this record.

## Two-scale model

A proposed research model is:

[
Y(t)=A(t)cos(2pi f_c t+phi)
]

where (A(t)) is the empirically constructed historical/narrative activity envelope and (f_c=8,Hz) is only a configurable reference carrier.

This is **HYP/SYM**, not EST.

## Validation protocol

1. Identify the exact saga edition/manuscript/source.
2. Extract explicit events without interpretation.
3. Tag each observation EST/DER/HYP/SYM/STOP/ERR.
4. Build the deep-structure graph.
5. Build the shadow register.
6. Cross-check against archaeology and independent written sources.
7. Construct time series only from dated or date-bounded observations.
8. Test Fourier/periodogram structure against shuffled/null data.
9. Repeat independently across multiple sagas and datasets.
10. Only then evaluate whether recurring temporal structure survives source bias and sampling effects.
