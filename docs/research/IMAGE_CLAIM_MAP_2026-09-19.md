- The existence of an atmospheric/climate research agreement would not, by itself, demonstrate deliberate chemical spraying from aircraft.

- domain: atmospheric physics / policy document verification

- falsification target: original agreement + annexes + implementation records + measured release mechanism + chemical/physical signatures

Source:

### IMG-006 — Persepolis / Darius foundation tablets

Observation:
- Image states that a roughly 2,500-year-old gold tablet was found beneath an ancient palace foundation at Persepolis.

Verified archaeological kernel:
- Darius I placed foundation deposits in the Apadana at Persepolis.
- Two recovered deposits contained paired gold and silver plaques with a trilingual foundation inscription.
- The plaques were recovered in 1933.
- The deposits belong to the Achaemenid period, around the early fifth century BCE, with exact dating of the deposit debated.

UPI:
- domain: archaeology / epigraphy
- status: EST for the foundation-deposit mechanism and inscriptions
- information-theoretic mapping: DER/SYM
- useful Ω1766 analogy: durable information is deliberately embedded in a physical boundary/foundation so that identity, authority and construction context survive later time

Important separation:

`archaeological foundation record -> EST`

`foundation as "entropy gate" -> SYM/DER analogy`

It is not evidence for a literal thermodynamic or electromagnetic gate.

## DNA write rule

No image-derived claim is promoted because:
- it looks convincing
- many people share it
- several images repeat the same number
- an AI-generated graphic labels it "quantum"
- a historical map contains a familiar place-name
- a symbolic relationship is mathematically elegant

Promotion requires source provenance plus the verification appropriate to the claim type.
