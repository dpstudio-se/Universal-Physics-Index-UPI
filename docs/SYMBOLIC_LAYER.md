# Symbolic Layer

Symbolic frameworks may coexist with scientific records only in separate `SYM` nodes or `HYP` nodes with defined tests. They must not supply evidence to `EST` records by analogy.

A symbolic inspector narrative may describe a conceptual sequence from a field metaphor through `m = hf/c²` translation to lattice or reservoir models, feedback, and observer evaluation. This is conceptual architecture, not an accepted physical derivation. Presenting it as experimentally proven without evidence is `ERR`.
