# Collaborative discovery: intuition, mirrors and evidence

UPI is most useful when it does more than classify finished claims. It should help a human turn a
cross-domain intuition into a typed, reversible and falsifiable map without confusing speed,
agreement or mathematical beauty with physical proof.

This document records a collaboration lesson. A human proposed that apparently different systems
could share a functional pattern across frequency, energy, mass, phase, surface response and
gravity. The first agent response concentrated on preventing false equivalence. That guard was
necessary, but by itself it reduced the work to a sequence of objections. Progress began when the
agent reconstructed the proposed whole, calculated the maps, ran them in both directions and used
units and edge cases to locate the exact boundaries.

The result was not a newly established theory. It was something useful and reproducible: a broad
intuition was compressed into a candidate mechanism, its lawful sub-maps, its failed identities
and the observations a real theory would have to recover.

## The two gears

### Discovery gear

Discovery gear is deliberately permissive about what may be explored. It:

- listens for the whole function before splitting it into claims;
- treats analogy as a search coordinate rather than evidence;
- calculates consequences under declared provisional assumptions;
- searches forward, backward and from the edges;
- keeps separate branches for ideas the human has not connected;
- looks for an established mathematical substrate underneath informal language; and
- returns a next calculation even when promotion is impossible.

### Ledger gear

Ledger gear is strict about what may be recorded as knowledge. It:

- types every quantity and records its units and domain;
- distinguishes energy, mass, frequency, entropy, force and geometry;
- names every additional assumption;
- carries the weakest status through a chain;
- separates mathematical checks, software tests, simulations and observations;
- records the first missing identity as `STOP`; and
- requires human review for promotion.

Neither gear works well alone. Discovery without the ledger produces attractive overclaims.
Ledger discipline without discovery can produce a correct but unhelpful refusal that never reaches
the testable core.

## The collaborative loop

Use this loop for a new conceptual proposal:

```text
human intent
  -> strongest coherent interpretation
  -> typed quantities and domains
  -> forward derivation A -> ... -> Z
  -> backward reconstruction Z -> ... -> A
  -> units, limits, branches and null model
  -> comparison with established maps and observations
  -> closed sub-chain + first STOP + next discriminating test
  -> human review
```

The backward path must do more than repeat the forward prose. Write the inverse functions, state
their domains and identify information discarded by non-invertible steps. If the reverse is defined
solely to undo the forward map, the round trip establishes internal consistency, not independent
evidence.

## Evidence can be inherited, but not invented

A digital tool will contain more theoretical maps than new experiments. It should not demand a new
laboratory observation for every exact rewrite.

Suppose established observations support quantities connected by maps within the same domain. An
exact composition can inherit that provenance when it introduces no new physical identification:

```text
existing observations
  + established relations
  + dimensionally valid composition
  + closed round trip
  = evidence-backed derivation in the declared domain
```

If the composition adds a mechanism, crosses domains, changes the meaning of a quantity or depends
on an unobserved object, that addition receives its own `DER`, `HYP`, `SYM` or `STOP` status. Absence
of falsification alone never creates `EST`.

An observation of an analogue system is evidence about that analogue. It can test a shared
mathematical mechanism, but it is not automatically an observation of the target system.

## Same function is not yet same thing

Repeated functional form is valuable because it tells the agent where to investigate. It does not
erase types. Two systems may share a differential equation while their variables carry different
units, mechanisms and boundary conditions.

For every proposed cross-domain bridge, build a table with:

| Question | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is mapped? | One typed source quantity and one typed target quantity |
| What remains invariant? | A number, interval, probability, action or information payload |
| What changes units? | An explicit constant or calibrated map |
| What is the inverse? | A function and its valid domain |
| What is lost? | Degeneracy, coarse-graining, phase, sign or other discarded information |
| What distinguishes identity from analogy? | A mechanism or observation unavailable to mere curve matching |
| What would fail? | A numerical, logical or observational falsification condition |

Record shared form as `FORM_SIMILAR`/`SYM` until a shared mechanism is demonstrated. Continue
calculating after that classification; `SYM` is a productive lead, not a command to stop.

## Failure analysis: where an agent can destroy useful work

### Braking before comprehension

Failure: the agent sees a speculative phrase, emits a status warning and never reconstructs the
proposed mechanism.

Cost: a potentially testable sub-chain remains hidden, and the human must repeat the whole idea in
greater detail.

Correction: state the strongest coherent interpretation first, then test it. Place the warning at
the exact failing link rather than in front of the entire exploration.

### Agreeing with the whole because one mirror closes

Failure: an algebraic inverse is treated as proof of the physical interpretation.

Cost: circular definitions can masquerade as discovery.

Correction: label what the mirror proves: type preservation, numerical reconstruction or
composability. Attach physical confidence only from independent provenance and observations.

### Connecting separate ideas without permission

Failure: the agent imports symbols or narratives from earlier discussion into a new proposal.

Cost: the map becomes impossible to audit and the owner has to disentangle claims the owner never
joined.

Correction: start a new branch when the user changes subject. Add a bridge only when the user names
it or an explicit mechanism requires it.

### Demanding speculative rescue objects

Failure: a chain is declared viable only if an undiscovered particle, dimension or constant later
appears.

Cost: the map becomes insulated from present failure.

Correction: test the core with known inputs. Put every unobserved dependency on a separate `HYP`
branch. The known core must remain inspectable without it.

### Tearing down the substrate instead of the bridge

Failure: one failed composition is described as if it invalidated all established nodes in the
chain.

Correction: preserve observations and established relations. Remove or quarantine the first bridge
that fails, record why, and start another route from the last safe node.

## Time compression without epistemic compression

Human-AI collaboration can compress the mechanical part of theoretical exploration. A model can
search formulas, normalize units, compose maps, calculate limiting cases and expose contradictions
in minutes. A human can supply intent, recognize meaningful shapes, reject irrelevant associations
and choose which boundary matters.

That speed is real, but it compresses the route to a well-formed hypothesis, not the historical time
needed for nature to supply independent evidence. Avoid both extremes:

- do not dismiss a result merely because it was found quickly;
- do not promote it merely because a century of algebra was traversed quickly.

The useful output of a short session is a smaller search space, an executable map, explicit
assumptions and a discriminating test.

## Example pattern: a surface analogy for emergent gravity

A fluid surface can motivate a candidate structure:

```text
microscopic degrees of freedom
  -> energy and entropy
  -> effective surface response
  -> curvature
  -> motion of disturbances
```

The analogy becomes scientifically useful when it exposes tests rather than when its nouns are
identified. A candidate gravity model must recover, from declared microstates rather than inserted
targets:

- the observed dimensional force law;
- relativistic covariance and the Einstein limit in its stated domain;
- the appropriate entropy-area relation;
- cosmological and local-gravity constraints; and
- at least one observable not used to tune the map.

If a literal two-spatial-dimensional force law produces the wrong radial scaling, that literal
bridge fails. A different branch, such as boundary information generating an effective bulk
description, may remain open. The failure narrows the hypothesis; it does not erase the originating
intuition.

Status of this example: the fluid and geometric equations used as inputs retain their source
statuses; shared form is `SYM`; a proposed common microscopic mechanism is `HYP`; a missing
non-circular microstate-to-gravity map is `STOP`.

## Compact agent protocol

When time is short, use seven lines:

```text
Intent: strongest coherent reading of the human's proposed whole
Map: typed A -> ... -> Z
Mirror: Z -> ... -> A, including domain and information loss
Closed: exact sub-chains and inherited provenance
Boundary: first failed identity, not a generic objection
Status: EST / DER / HYP / STOP / ERR / SYM per claim
Next: smallest calculation or observation that discriminates the proposal
```

The governing maxim is:

> Explore freely, type every quantity, verify hard, and claim exactly what the loop and its
> evidence establish.

This is collaborative intelligence in operational form: the human does not hand the agent a finished
theory, and the agent does not replace the human with a verdict. Together they construct a map that
can survive contact with algebra, code, prior evidence and eventually observation.
